Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Nathaniel Nigussie ("we", "our", or "us") built the Ethiopian Calendar mobile application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the Service.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you. The Service is designed to work fully on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The Ethiopian Calendar app does not collect, request, or store any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email address, phone number, or any contact details)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or unique installation identifiers
  • Photographs, videos, or files
  • Camera, microphone, or contacts access

Any data you enter into the app for your personal use (such as events, reminders, or birthdays) is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

2. Third-Party Advertising

The app may display advertisements served by a third-party advertising network such as Google AdMob. When ads are shown, the third-party ad provider may collect certain information — such as device identifiers, IP address, and advertising-related data — in accordance with its own privacy policy, in order to serve and measure advertisements.

For more information about how Google processes data when you use AdMob, please review the Google Privacy & Terms page.

3. Children's Privacy

The Service does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. As we do not collect any personal data at all, children's privacy is protected by design.

4. Data Security

Because we do not collect or store any personal data, there is no personal data that could be exposed or breached. Any locally stored settings and user-created events remain on your device and are protected by your device's own security measures.

5. Permissions

The app may request certain permissions (such as notification or alarm scheduling) strictly for the core functionality of the Service, such as reminding you of events you create. These permissions are used solely to provide the features you request and are not used to collect any personal data.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. You are encouraged to review this policy periodically for any changes.
