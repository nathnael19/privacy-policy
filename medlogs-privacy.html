Privacy Policy

App Name: MedLogs  |  Effective Date: October 8, 2026

Nathnael ("we", "our", or "us") built the MedLogs app as a free/freemium personal health log and vital signs tracker. This page is used to inform users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our App.

Core Privacy Commitment: MedLogs is designed with your privacy in mind. Your vital signs, health logs, and personal medical records are stored securely and primarily on your device.

1. Information Collection and Use

To provide a robust health tracking experience, MedLogs may collect or process the following types of information:

  • Health & Vital Signs Data: Information you input manually or via connected logs (such as blood pressure, heart rate, temperature, blood glucose, symptoms, and notes). This data is stored locally on your device using encrypted/local databases (Room).
  • Device Permissions:
    • Notifications: Used solely to send you medication or health logging reminders you schedule.
    • Biometrics (Fingerprint/Face Unlock): Used locally on your device to secure access to your sensitive health logs. We never store or transmit your biometric data.
    • Camera/Storage: Used when you attach reports or export/import your JSON backup files.
  • Third-Party Services: The app may utilize Firebase Services (such as App Check and Crashlytics) or Gemini AI features for trend analysis. These services may collect anonymous diagnostic data to improve app stability and security.

2. Data Security & Storage

We value your trust in providing your health data. All vital logs and health records remain on your local device storage unless you explicitly export or share a backup report. You maintain full control over your data through local JSON export and import features.

3. Children's Privacy

MedLogs is not directed to anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
